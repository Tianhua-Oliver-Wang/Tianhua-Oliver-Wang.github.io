About

My interests lie at the intersection of climate finance and public policy, with a particular focus on project-level green infrastructure financing and risk pricing.

I am interested in how policy influences the allocation of capital, how climate-related risks enter financing decisions, and how these mechanisms affect green infrastructure investment.
